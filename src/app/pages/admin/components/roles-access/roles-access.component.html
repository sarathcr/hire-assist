Roles and Access

Permission Request: Launch Sync Runner

HireAssist will synchronize your user accounts, roles, departments, and designations directly with OpenOrg.

Why is this permission needed?
OpenOrg operates securely on the internal office network (192.168.3.75), which external cloud servers cannot reach directly. Continuing authorizes HireAssist to invoke the local sync runner on your machine using your network connection.
Automated Sync: Fetches all active employees from OpenOrg and provisions or updates their profiles and roles in HireAssist.
Protected Accounts: Super Admins and Candidates are preserved and will not be altered.
Safe Deactivation: Organization users no longer active in OpenOrg will be safely deactivated.
Active Recruitment Guard: Users currently assigned to active recruitments will not be removed or interrupted.
Prerequisites: Please ensure your device is connected to the office network (WiFi/LAN or VPN). When prompted by your browser, click "Open HireAssist Sync Bridge".
